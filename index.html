Meal Planner — Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Meal Planner is a household meal-planning app. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

What data the app stores

Recipes, your meal plan, pantry contents, and shopping lists that you enter into the app. This data is stored in your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, not on any server we operate.

Household sharing

If you invite someone to your household, your recipes, meal plan, pantry, and shopping list are shared with them through Apple's iCloud sharing — visible only to people you explicitly invite, never public.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party or company
  • No account or sign-up is required beyond your existing Apple ID / iCloud

Notifications

Meal prep reminders are scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's local notification framework. Nothing about your meal plan is sent to a server to generate these.

Recipe import

When you import a recipe from a URL, the app fetches that page directly from your device to parse it. The URL you provide isn't sent anywhere except to that recipe website itself.

Siri & Shortcuts

Siri and Shortcuts integration (e.g. adding a shopping list item by voice) is processed on-device via Apple's frameworks.
